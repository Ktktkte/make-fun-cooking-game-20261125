Entry for make.fun's "Make the best cooking game" bounty: **The Night Shift Diner** — a time-management cooking game. Customers take a seat at the counter and pin their order on the board; cook each dish station by station (Grill → Toppings → Plate), serve it before patience runs out, and survive the 3-minute shift. Fast service earns tips; a customer who waits too long storms out and costs reputation.

## Play it

Serve the game folder locally, then open it in a desktop browser:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8132 --directory game
# open http://127.0.0.1:8132/index.html
```

Click an order ticket, then click the stations in sequence (or press 1–4). Press Space — or click the plate — to serve a ready dish.

## Hand it in

```sh
node main.ts
```
